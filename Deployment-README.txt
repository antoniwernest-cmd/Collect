ADVENTURE READING — DEPLOYMENT

Upload the contents of this folder to a static website host. index.html is at the root. Keep the four story folders and assets folder together. No build step is required.

Includes Soap Box Racer, Obstacle Course, Canoe Trip and Rock Collecting at E, F, G, L, M, N, P and R. E has short CVC-rich passages. P has ten substantial reading pages. Voice is disabled until a teacher enters 259 and chooses Enable voice; independent text and choices remain silent.

Illustrations.html previews the Pixar-style artwork. Illustration-Prompts.json records the built-in image-generation prompts and final asset paths.

The story remains visible beside all answer tools. Short activities share one screen; smaller screens switch only the answer area. The teacher/parent PDF includes the complete original story text for every reading page as searchable print text, plus recorded responses and marks.
